About the operator

A little context behind the code.

I’m Narayani Chaudhary (NC), an M.Tech researcher in Information Technology (Cyber Security) at Indira Gandhi Delhi Technical University for Women (IGDTUW).

My interests sit at the intersection of digital forensics, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence and privacy-aware machine learning. I enjoy turning research ideas into practical prototypes, investigation workflows and tools that make security analysis more explainable.

My current work explores forensic automation, threat analysis, evidence integrity, anti-forensic detection and federated learning. I’m especially interested in building systems that are useful to investigators—not just impressive in a demo.
